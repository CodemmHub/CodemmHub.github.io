About

01 · Background

I started in design, not code. Years of building brand identities and visual systems for clients taught me how something should feel before I ever touched a line of JavaScript. Now I build the sites and systems that carry those identities too, full-stack, from the ground up.

I'm not choosing between design and development. I use both, depending on what the problem needs.

Freelance since, working across brand strategy, web development, and digital campaigns, plus consulting on AI-era digital presence for brands still running on old playbooks. Currently completing a BSc in Computer Science, aimed at advanced AI research after graduation.
