About

01 · Background

Nature taught me to see structure and shape first. Code just gave me a faster way to build with it.

I started in design, not code. Years of building brand identities and visual systems for clients taught me how something should feel before I ever touched a line of JavaScript. Now I build the sites and systems that carry those identities too, full-stack, from the ground up.

I'm not choosing between design and development. I use both, depending on what the problem needs.

Freelancing gives me the freedom to keep choosing different problems instead of settling into one: brand strategy one project, full-stack builds the next, digital campaigns after that, plus consulting on AI-era digital presence for brands still running on old playbooks. That same pull toward new problems is why I'm finishing a BSc in Computer Science, with AI research next.
